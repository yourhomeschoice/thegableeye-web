What to check before buying a universal travel adapter

A universal travel adapter can be useful across many countries, but plug compatibility and electrical compatibility are not the same thing.

Travel adapters allow a device's plug to connect to different socket types. They do not necessarily convert the voltage supplied by the electrical outlet.

Before travelling, check the voltage information on your devices and chargers. Make sure the equipment you intend to connect is suitable for the electrical supply at your destination.

This is particularly important for higher-powered electrical equipment and appliances that may not support different international voltage standards.

Also remember that universal travel adapters are not necessarily compatible with every socket in every country. Check the manufacturer's destination coverage and plug specifications before purchasing.

For devices such as phones, tablets and laptops, confirm that the adapter's available USB output is appropriate for the devices you intend to charge, particularly when several are connected simultaneously.
